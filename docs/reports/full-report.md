# Regional industrial symbiosis in Västra Götaland County — results (2022)

This report applies the same core method as the companion Skaraborg study to
all 49 kommuner in Västra Götaland County. Unless stated otherwise, figures
refer to the **baseline** scenario (MAESTRI plus reviewed W2RKG matching,
plausibility exclusions and corrected manufacturing scaling) in the
**mass** regime. Explore the <a href="../">interactive county report</a> for
kommun, sub-region, industry, product and scenario detail. See also the
<a href="../method.html">method and limitations</a> and the
<a href="https://makijota.github.io/skaraborg-symbiosis/skaraborg/">Skaraborg case</a>.

## 1. The county in numbers

| | Skaraborg | Västra Götaland | VG / Skaraborg |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Kommuner | 15 | 49 | |
| Workers (SCB, all sectors) | 133,700 | 954,823 | 7.1× |
| Share of Swedish jobs | 2.4 % | 16.9 % | |
| Share of Swedish manufacturing jobs | 4.7 % | 20.3 % | |
| Registry firms (sectors A–E) | 706 | 2,811 | 4.0× |
| Waste generated, sectors A–F (W) | 2.0 Mt | 10.7 Mt | 5.3× |
| Imported production inputs (D) | 0.46 Mt, 2.5 bn SEK | 12.3 Mt, 99 bn SEK | 27× |
| D without mineral fuels (CN 27) | 0.40 Mt | 1.83 Mt | 4.6× |
| Apparent regional material use (stage 13) | 8.6 Mt | 41.5 Mt | 4.8× |

- **Waste (10.7 Mt).** Mining and quarrying (B) gives 5.6 Mt, construction (F)
  2.4 Mt, waste management (E) 1.2 Mt and manufacturing (C) 1.1 Mt.
  Göteborg (3.1 Mt), Mölndal (1.1 Mt), Lidköping (0.75 Mt) and Stenungsund
  (0.72 Mt) generate the most. In all four, the largest item is quarry waste
  (NACE 0812), which the model estimates at 4.3 Mt for the county. As in
  Skaraborg, this is probably overestimated (§6).
- **Demand (12.3 Mt) is 85 % crude oil.** Preemraff Lysekil (6.8 Mt CN 2709)
  and the Göteborg refineries (3.2 Mt) dominate the imported inputs. Mineral
  fuels make up 10.5 Mt of D and 85 bn SEK of its value. The scenario
  **no_energy_inputs** removes CN 27 from demand. It is the better reference
  for what waste can replace, because crude oil cannot be replaced by any
  industrial waste in the matching databases.

## 2. Symbiosis potential by scale

Mass regime. Value is the SEK of the replaced inputs at the county's unit values.

| Scale | Baseline: allocated | % of W | % of D (mass) | MSEK | No CN 27: allocated | % of D (mass) | MSEK | % of D (value) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1b Region, LP optimum | 592 kt | 5.5 | 4.8 | 2,998 | 456 kt | 24.9 | 1,220 | 8.4 |
| 2a Kommun, intra only | 324 kt | 3.0 | 2.6 | 1,814 | 257 kt | 14.0 | 842 | 5.8 |
| **2c Sub-regions on their own** | 468 kt | 4.4 | 3.8 | 2,356 | 379 kt | 20.7 | 1,143 | 7.9 |
| 2b Kommun, intra + inter | 589 kt | 5.5 | 4.8 | 3,012 | 453 kt | 24.7 | 1,233 | 8.5 |
| 3b Firms, top-down | 475 kt | 6.6 | 3.9 | 2,439 | 354 kt | 4.9* | 926 | 6.6 |

\* For firm scales the first ratio is the share of firm-level W (7.2 Mt).

- **Greedy reaches the LP optimum** at the regional scale (592 = 592 kt), as in
  Skaraborg. The kommun two-stage allocation (2b) recovers 99.5 % of it.
- **Exchange between kommuner nearly doubles local use.** Intra-kommun
  exchange gives 324 kt. Exchange within each sub-region adds 144 kt (2c), and
  exchange across the whole county adds a further 121 kt (2b). Of the 2b flows,
  55 % stay within a kommun.
- **Firm-to-firm links are long.** The mean exchange distance is 76 km
  (top-down basis; 64 km bottom-up), against 34–36 km in Skaraborg. The nearest compatible
  buyer of many streams is in another sub-region.
- **Waste replacing fuels.** About 135 kt of waste replaces fuels in the
  baseline, and those pairs carry most of the value (§6). Without CN 27, the
  substituted value falls from 3.0 to 1.2 bn SEK, but its share of the
  (fuel-free) input bill rises to 8.5 %, close to Skaraborg's 8.7 %.

What is substituted (baseline, 2b): crushed stone from quarry overburden
(117 kt), fuel wood from bark, wood waste and pallets (100 kt), petroleum oils
and gas from oily wastes (132 kt, see §6), soil → salt (49 kt, see §6),
concrete articles from construction mixtures (46 kt), limestone from concrete
(29 kt), and recovered paper (17 kt). Recovered paper, aluminium scrap and
recovered-fibre pulp are small in tonnes but among the most valuable per
tonne.

## 3. Sub-regions: alone and together

Kommun two-stage allocation, mass regime, baseline.

| Sub-region | W (kt) | D (kt) | Own waste used, alone | Own waste used, in county | D met, alone | D met, in county |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Göteborgsregionen (12) | 5,931 | 4,074 | 3.2 % | 5.8 % | 4.6 % | 4.9 % |
| Skaraborg (15) | 2,007 | 461 | 8.2 % | 6.2 % | 35.8 % | 39.5 % |
| Fyrbodal (14) | 1,577 | 7,623 | 4.8 % | 5.4 % | 1.0 % | 1.4 % |
| Sjuhärad (8) | 1,212 | 152 | 3.3 % | 3.1 % | 25.9 % | 65.8 % |

- **Göteborgsregionen is the county's supplier.** It sends 160 kt to the other
  sub-regions and receives 19 kt. Its main exports are 63 kt to Fyrbodal (oily
  wastes to the Lysekil refinery), 61 kt to Skaraborg (minerals, wood) and
  37 kt to Sjuhärad.
- **Sjuhärad gains the most from the county.** Its demand met by waste rises
  from 26 % on its own to 66 % in the county, mostly from Göteborgsregionen
  and Fyrbodal.
- **Skaraborg is the most self-contained sub-region.** On its own it uses
  8.2 % of its waste (165 kt), which reproduces the Skaraborg study exactly
  (consistency check). Inside the county it uses only 6.2 % (124 kt), because
  larger waste streams from Göteborgsregionen (61 kt) take part of its own
  demand first. Its demand met by waste still rises to 39.5 %. The greedy rule
  serves the largest waste first, so it is a ranking, not a market. Whether
  Göteborg's surplus should displace Skaraborg's own is a policy question,
  not a model result.
- **Flows between sub-regions are 37 % of the county's exchanged tonnes** (219
  of 589 kt) and 33 % of the value. Without CN 27, Göteborgsregionen's oily
  wastes no longer go to the Lysekil refinery (Göteborgsregionen → Fyrbodal
  drops from 63 to 12 kt). Flows between sub-regions are then 34 % of the
  tonnes but only 21 % of the value: what crosses sub-region borders is
  mostly bulky, low-value minerals and wood.

sub-regions on their own and within the county.

The ranking of uncertainties is the same as in Skaraborg. The matching
database moves the result most (224–859 kt, 0.5–4.9 bn SEK), ahead of quarry
waste. One difference: in the county, quarry waste matters less for the
substituted tonnes. Without it, all sources still allocate 687 kt, because
demand for crushed stone (the main outlet of overburden) is small relative to
the other streams.

| Domestic extraction | 7.2 Mt | 23.6 Mt (stone/gravel 17.1, biomass 6.3) |

| Lorry inflow / outflow | 3.7 / 3.8 Mt | 7.6 / 10.5 Mt |

| Baseline, no CN 27 in use | — | 4,549 kt (18.3 % of 24.8 Mt) |

Against what the county actually uses, not just what it imports, demand stops
being the limit. Waste could replace 6–18 % of material use, depending on
whether quarry waste and fuels are counted. Only lorry freight is in the
survey. Göteborg's port, rail and pipeline flows are not, so inflows and
outflows of the county are understated, more so than for Skaraborg.

## 6. Caveats specific to the county

1. **Three LLM-extension pairs are implausible at county scale** and should be
   added to `00_data/match_exclusions.csv`, or reviewed. They moved almost
   nothing in Skaraborg because the demand was not there:
   - `130507` oily water from separators → `2710` petroleum oils: 75 kt,
     704 MSEK. The stream is mostly water.
   - `160708` wastes containing oil → `2711` petroleum gas: 47 kt, 871 MSEK.
   - `170504` soil and stones → `2501` salt: 49 kt.

   Together they are 171 kt (29 %) of the baseline tonnes and 1.6 bn SEK
   (54 %) of its value. Excluding them changes Skaraborg's baseline by 2.3 kt.
   The `no_energy_inputs` scenario already removes the first two.
2. **Petroleum support services get the mining beta.** NACE 0910 has 63
   workers in VG (Stenungsund 33, Göteborg 26), mostly service firms around
   the chemical cluster and refineries. With beta_B = 11,504 t/worker they
   generate 0.72 Mt of "mining waste". This is the same flaw as the quarry
   beta (one B intensity for all of 05–09). The `quarry_beta*` scenarios
   rescale NACE 08 but not 09.
3. **Quarry waste**: 4.8 Mt modelled, against an SGU-implied quarry output of
   15.9 Mt for the county's 421 quarry workers (30 %). The overestimate is
   about 3–10×, as in Skaraborg.
4. **The firm registry covers sectors A–E only.** It includes 92 % of
   manufacturing workers and none in construction (F, 2.4 Mt of waste). F
   waste therefore reaches the firm scale only through the "not covered"
   totals.
5. **Ten firm locations were imputed** because coordinates were missing or
   more than 15 km outside their registered kommun. Firm identifiers and
   underlying location records are not included in this published report.

## 7. Priorities for further work

- Review the three candidate waste-product matches described in §6.1.
- Use `no_energy_inputs` (or exclude CN 27 everywhere) as the county's
  reference for imported demand, and report CN 27 separately.
- Give NACE 09 its own intensity, or fold it into C/services, as was done for
  quarries.
