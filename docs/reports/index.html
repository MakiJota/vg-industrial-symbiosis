Regional industrial symbiosis in Västra Götaland

Summary of 2022 model estimates across 49 kommuner. Use the interactive report to explore industries, waste types, products, locations and scenarios.

At a glance

  • The study estimates 10.7 Mt of waste from sectors A–F and 12.3 Mt of imported production inputs. Mineral fuels, mainly crude oil, account for 10.5 Mt of those inputs.
  • In the baseline kommun-level allocation, 589 kt of waste is matched to compatible demand. Excluding mineral fuels from demand gives 453 kt.
  • Across scenarios, allocated mass varies substantially with the matching database and assumptions. The “all sources, reviewed” scenario gives 750 kt; quarry-waste and matching assumptions remain important uncertainties.
  • Västra Götaland is also reported as four sub-regions: Göteborgsregionen, Fyrbodal, Sjuhärad and Skaraborg.
