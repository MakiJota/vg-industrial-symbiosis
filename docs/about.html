About this study

This study estimates how industrial waste generated in Västra Götaland County could replace raw and semi-finished materials that businesses otherwise buy. It covers the county's 49 kommuner and uses 2022 as its reference year.

The interactive report presents results at county, kommun, sub-region and firm scales. The Skaraborg study is available as a companion case, analysed with the same core approach.

Sources and scope

The analysis combines workplace employment and trade statistics from Statistics Sweden (SCB), waste and employment statistics from Eurostat, domestic extraction accounts, lorry freight data from Trafikanalys, documented industrial exchanges in MAESTRI, and reviewed waste-to-resource matches from W2RKG.

These are model estimates, not measured exchanges or forecasts. Import data do not show materials bought from Swedish suppliers, and estimated substitution assumes one tonne of waste can replace one tonne of product. The report describes possibilities to investigate, not verified business opportunities.
